Keyboard shortcuts

Enter or Space
Press the prompt's main button, marked ↵
1–9, 0
Press the button marked with that number; with a card open, its actions
A
Attack with every creature that can
E
End your turn, or pass to your next turn
← ↑ → ↓
Step through the cards on the table and in hand; Enter opens the one in focus
L
Open or close the log, under the turn
Esc
Close the open card, the log, this list or the settings
Tab
Move between buttons
?
Show this list

Hold Alt to see each button’s key. No shortcut fires while you type in a box.
