# Tarkir: Dragonstorm census

The Tarkir: Dragonstorm (TDM) cards not yet in the pool, and which wait on a
capability. Derived 2026-11-15 from MTGJSON's set list against `data/cards/`
(#7693). The set list is `data/decks/sets/tdm/all.txt`, which the suite reads,
so every card of the set stays in the pool.

TDM has 276 card names (MTGJSON 5.3.0+20261114; 289 face names; 258
`normal`, 13 `adventure`, 5 `saga`, its 6 `reversible_card` printings being
alternate frames of cards already counted); 46 were already in the pool, 230
missing (241 faces).

| Place | Cards |
| --- | --- |
| Added by the census (#7715) | 36 |
| Batches #7704 to #7713 | 187 |
| Capability issues #7697 to #7703 | 7 |

- Trend: the first dry run translated 36 of the 230 missing; 7 needed a
  capability.
- Translate reads no Omen (`adventure`) or Saga card, so the 16 such names
  missing went to batches by reading. The translator was not widened.
- Batches 3, 5, 7 and 9 were blocked by batch 2 (#7705), which wrote the
  Dragonstorm self-bounce and the white Monk prowess token first.

## Decks

None is committed: decks are optional, so `data/decks/sets/tdm/` holds only
the set list, 276 names, each Omen card by MTGJSON's joined name.

## How it went

Finished 2026-11-15.

| Route | Cards | PRs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Translated (census) | 36 | #7715 |
| Batches | 187 | #7716 (19), #7717 (19), #7723 (18), #7720 (20), #7722 (20), #7719 (18), #7724 (18), #7718 (19), #7725 (17), #7721 (19) |
| Behind a capability | 7, plus 1 from another set | below |

- Census trend: the first dry run translated 36 of the 230 missing; 7
  needed a capability. No batch sent a card on to a capability.
- Capability issues, hardest first: #7697 Call the Spirit Dragons (#7731),
  #7698 Sidisi, Regent of the Mire (#7729), #7699 Surrak, Elusive Hunter
  (#7730), #7700 Windcrag Siege (#7728, adding Isshin, Two Heavens as One),
  #7701 Stalwart Successor (#7727), #7702 Hundred-Battle Veteran (#7735),
  #7703 Molten Exhale (#7732).
- Fix issues: #7734 (Sidisi's ability offered when no X makes both its cost
  and its target legal, #7737).
- Filed along the way, each open and off the tracker: #7726 (keeping a
  creature attacking from causing abilities to trigger, synthetic), #7733 (a
  spell's cost announcing X for a criterion, Nahiri's Sacrifice), #7736 (the
  spell side of #7734, Stir the Grave).
- Audits: 4 of 7 capability PRs; 1 found rules defects. Reviewed inline
  #7727, #7730 and #7732 and the fix PR #7737; skipped the census and all ten
  batches, each recorded on #7692.
  - #7729: the ability was offered when no X made both cost and target legal
    (#7734); its "no printing needs it" deferral was refuted (#7733).
  - #7735: no rules defect; a haddock splice in `Parun.Engine.Cost`
    (`addsToItsCast` between `permissionsOf`'s comment and signature), fixed
    by the finish.
- `data/printings.json`: regenerated by the finish, adding 625 printings (350
  of them TDM) of cards added since the 2026-11-14 dump and dropping none.
- Oracle text: the finish's `parun ingest` run named no pool card as
  disagreeing with MTGJSON. It would also have ingested 11 keyword-only Star
  Trek Commander cards; they were left out.
- Cost: 4098 turns, 478.3M cache read, 6.1M cache write, 2287k output over
  all 19 unit PRs, 568 session-minutes in all. The finish unit's is on its
  own PR. Wall clock ~5.3h, from the tracker's opening to the last unit's
  merge (#7737), then the finish.
- Owner review:
  - #7731: decisions.md "A Dragon chosen for two colours gets its counters
    in one placement"; `PutCounters` gained a `slot` field.
  - #7728: edited the decisions.md entry "Which abilities a creature
    attacking causes to trigger"; `EntriesTrigger` gained an `Attacking`
    move.
  - Wire-format changes: #7727 (`CounterTrigger.frequency`), #7730
    (`BecomesTargetedTrigger.zones`), #7732 (`CastAsThoughItHadFlash` gained
    cost components), #7735 (`CastFromGraveyard` gained `entersWith`;
